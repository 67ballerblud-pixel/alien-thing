ALIEN PACK
==========

1) SKIN (works for everyone who sees you, no mod needed)
   skin/alien_classic.png  - 4px arms (Steve model)
   skin/alien_slim.png     - 3px arms (Alex model)
   Upload at minecraft.net -> Profile -> Skin (pick Classic / Slim to match the file).
   A skin cannot change head size/shape, so this is the blocky version.

2) FIGURA AVATAR (big head, thin torso, long skinny arms like the video)
   Needs the Figura mod (Fabric). Copy the "Alien" folder to:
       .minecraft/figura/avatars/Alien
   In game: open the Figura menu (default key: K, may clash with DonutPay settings -> rebind one),
   pick "Alien" and upload/equip. Only players who also run Figura see the avatar;
   everyone else sees your normal skin (set the skin above to match).
   Tweak the proportions in Alien/script.lua (the setScale lines).

preview.png shows the skin normal and with the avatar proportions.
